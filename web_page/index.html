DCUB – Differential Codon Usage Bias is a software tool designed to provide researchers with convenient access to the computational model. Featuring a user-friendly graphical interface with advanced configuration options, DCUB enables users to easily tailor the optimization process to suit specific experimental or research needs. The sample screenshot demonstrates DCUB’s integrated workflow that enables researchers to efficiently perform parameterized optimization for their gene of interest and retrieve comprehensive, interpretable results.
